PDF Fit by CTRX

Privacy policy

18 November 2026

Files you choose

PDF Fit reads the names and contents of PDFs and photos you select through the Android file picker to compress, preview and save them. Processing happens on the device. PDF Fit does not transmit files or device data to CTRX or other parties and has no accounts, analytics or ads.

Storage and deletion

Working copies are stored in the app’s private cache. PDF Fit removes the previous import when you select new files and clears working copies older than 24 hours on a fresh launch. You can clear the cache any time in Android Settings. Exported PDFs remain in the destination you choose until you delete them.

Cloud providers

If you select a cloud provider in Android’s file picker, that provider handles the transfer under its own privacy policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the App support contact on the PDF Fit listing on Google Play.
